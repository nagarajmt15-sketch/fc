# Forest Cafe — Final Asset Map

This corrects and finalizes the earlier plan now that every image has actually been inspected.

## ForestCafeIMG — 26 real photos (`assets/cafe/cafe-01.jpg` … `cafe-26.jpg`)
Genuinely varied: entrance/signage, indoor counter, outdoor mosaic fire-pit seating,
covered dining, the stone bridge/pond feature, wall art tucked into trees, a kids' play
corner, and both day and evening shots. Used across Hero, background of the Soil→Sip
sequence, and ambient texture — **not** just the first 1–2 images.

## MENU — 11 images (`assets/menu/menu-01.jpg` … `menu-11.jpg`)
This folder is menu + retail, not just plated food: a real chalkboard menu, plated
food (sandwich, flatbread, cake), filter coffee, a cold coffee, the retail shelf, and
bottled/pouched products with the Forest Cafe label. All used in the "From the Counter"
carousel; two product shots (`menu-09`) doubled into the Little Farmer section since
they visually represent "what's made from what's grown."

## Extra → reframed as **Art & Handcrafted Details** — 9 images (`assets/art/art-01.jpg` … `art-09.jpg`)
Correction from the earlier plan: this is not a formal art gallery. It's a mix of
brand/signage material (highway banner, entrance sign, Instagram story graphic,
business card) and genuinely handcrafted décor (a painted chalkboard mural, mossy
sculpted face planters on the garden wall). The site frames this honestly as
**"The Art Behind the Forest" / handcrafted touches**, not invented gallery pieces.
8 of the 9 are used in the masonry grid; the Instagram story graphic was left out as
it's a duplicate of brand messaging already carried by the logo/hero.

## FarmingIMG → reframed as **Little Farmer** — 2 images (`assets/farmer/farmer-01.jpg`, `farmer-02.jpg`)
Correction: both images are cascading hanging-planter greenery at the cafe, not
farmland or crops. Copy was written to say "what grows here" rather than implying a
working farm, so nothing is overclaimed. Both images are used — neither is dropped.

## LOGO — 1 file
Transparent PNG, white linework — used as-is in the hero, inverted for legibility on
dark backgrounds where needed.

## OwnerIMG / TonyCotIMG — confirmed empty
Both replaced with **hand-drawn SVG placeholders** built directly into the page
(not a stock photo, not a generated "fake photo," and not a cafe image reused). Each
is visually distinct, labeled ("Concept Preview" / "A real portrait will take the
place of this concept…"), and structured so a real photo can drop straight into
`assets/owner/` or `assets/tinycot/` and replace the placeholder `<div>` with an
`<img>` — no other markup changes needed.

## Typography
`Coffee Town.ttf` / `.otf` — used only for headings/display text (hero title, section
titles, big statements). Body copy uses the system sans-serif stack, per the brief.

## What changed from the original plan
- "Little Farmer" copy softened from "our farm" to "what grows around us" — the real
  images don't support a farmland narrative.
- "Art" section copy softened from "creative works" to "handcrafted details" — the
  folder is mostly brand signage plus a couple of genuine handmade pieces, not a
  gallery.
- Menu folder confirmed to include retail products (bottles, coffee pouches), so it's
  presented as "From the Counter" rather than a food-only menu.
